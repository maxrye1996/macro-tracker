TrackRyte Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 August 2026
TrackRyte is made by get-a-site.co.uk.

The short version: everything you log in TrackRyte stays on your device.
We cannot see it, we do not collect it, and we have no server that could
receive it.


1. What TrackRyte is

TrackRyte is a daily tracking app. You choose what to track (for example
calories, water or a medication dose), set your own targets, and log
values by hand. It is available as an Android app, an iOS app, and a web
app.


2. The mobile app collects no data

The Android and iOS apps have no account system, no sign-up, and no
analytics. They make no network requests of any kind. This is enforced
technically, not just promised: the app ships with a Content Security
Policy of "connect-src 'none'", which means the app is blocked by the
operating system's own webview from talking to any server, including
ours.

Everything you enter - trackers, targets and logged values - is stored
in local storage on your device only. Deleting the app deletes this
data. We never receive a copy at any point.


3. The website and web version

The web version of TrackRyte works the same way: your trackers, targets
and logged values are stored in your browser's local storage on your
device and are never sent anywhere.

The website uses Vercel Analytics to count anonymous page views. This
records that a page was visited, but uses no cookies and creates no
profile, and it never includes anything you log in the app. We use these
counts only to see whether anyone is visiting the site.

Our hosting provider (Vercel Inc.) receives the standard technical
information any web server receives when you load a page, such as your
IP address. This is used to deliver the page and is subject to Vercel's
privacy policy.


4. Backups and exports

TrackRyte can export your data as a CSV file so you can back it up or
move to a new phone. That file is created on your device and saved
wherever you choose. What happens to it after that is in your hands - we
never see it. If you store it with a cloud service, that service's
privacy policy applies to the copy you put there.


5. Data sharing

We do not sell, share, trade or transfer any personal data, because we
do not hold any. There is nothing to hand over, even if asked.


6. Children

TrackRyte does not collect personal data from anyone, including
children.


7. Your rights

Under UK GDPR and similar laws you have rights over personal data an
organisation holds about you. TrackRyte holds none: your data is on
your device, under your direct control. You can view it in the app,
export it as CSV, or erase it permanently with the "Delete all data"
button in Settings (or by uninstalling the app).


8. Changes to this policy

If TrackRyte ever changes in a way that affects privacy - which would
go against the reason it was built - this policy will be updated and
the effective date above will change. The current version is always at
this address.
